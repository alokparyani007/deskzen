## 4. How it works (3 steps)
1. **Find your best posture.** Sit comfortably tall and hold still for 3 seconds. SitGlow remembers what "great" looks like for you.
2. **Work like normal.** SitGlow quietly watches for leaning in, head drops, and tense shoulders, right in your browser.
3. **Get a gentle nudge.** When you've slipped for a while, you'll get a kind reminder. Tap "Fixed it" and watch your score glow back up.

## 5. Features (6 cards)
1. **Live posture score:** A simple 0-100 score compared with your own best posture.
2. **Gentle nudges:** Kind, specific tips like "Lean back a little," never nagging.
3. **Smart break reminders:** Move, stretch, and refill your water at the right moments.
4. **Guided stretches:** One-to-three-minute routines for your neck, shoulders, back, and wrists.
5. **Screen-distance check:** Know when you've crept too close to your screen, in inches.
6. **Progress and streaks:** Daily scores, weekly trends, and streaks that keep you going.
