# Prompt 09: Posture Print calibration + Desk Sense

You are working in the DeskZen codebase (formerly SitGlow): a browser PWA built with Vite, React, TypeScript and Tailwind that uses MediaPipe Tasks Vision (Pose Landmarker + Face Landmarker with iris) on a front-facing webcam. Everything runs on-device. No video or images may ever be uploaded, saved, or logged.

GOAL
Replace the current calibration with "Posture Print": a guided, about one-minute calibration for SITTING and for STANDING, a silent daily "Zen Check", automatic re-anchoring when the camera or desk changes, and reliable sit/stand switching ("Desk Sense"). Keep the existing posture engine, scoring, EMA smoothing, the 30-second sustained rule, cooldowns, and storage layer. Add to them; do not rewrite them.

BEFORE YOU CODE
1. Read the codebase and list, in plain language, where these live today: camera setup, MediaPipe setup, the posture engine and its signals, the current calibration screen (silhouette, coaching pill, quick-tip chips, 5 s hold ring, median baseline), storage of baselines, work-style setting (Sit only / Sit and stand / Stand only), and the current sit/stand detection.
2. Propose a short plan with the files you will add or change. Then implement it in small steps, keeping the app working after each step.
3. Do NOT rename existing storage keys. Add a "version" field and a migration so existing users' baselines keep working and are treated as a low-confidence Posture Print until they re-check.

USER-FACING FLOW (first run, sitting; target about 65 s total)
A. Privacy card before the browser camera prompt: "DeskZen learns your good posture in about a minute. Your camera stays on this device. No video is saved or sent." Button: "Turn on camera". If permission is denied, show plain steps to turn it back on in the browser, and a Try again button.
B. Setup Check (up to 20 s): live mirror + soft outline. Four chips that turn emerald when passed: Framing (head and both shoulders in frame, head in the upper-middle area), Light (face not too dark, not backlit), Level (camera not looking far up or down at her, from face pitch), Distance (iris-based estimate, about arm's length). Show ONE coaching pill for the biggest blocker only. Show a Setup Score 0-100. After 20 s show "Good enough, keep going" so nobody is blocked.
C. Body Reset (about 20 s): four steps, one caption at a time, each with a small animated outline of a woman: "Scoot your hips all the way back. Let the chair hold you." / "Roll your shoulders up, back, and let them drop." / "Glide your chin straight back." / "Breathe in... and slowly out." Detect the shoulder roll (shoulders rise then fall) and tick it live. Voice is optional, uses window.speechSynthesis and prefers voices where voice.localService is true; captions are always shown. Include a "Skip movements" option for anyone who can't do them.
D. Lock (5 s): a small emerald orb follows the smoothed head center; a target ring around the reset position. When the orb is inside the ring and she is still, the ring fills amber to emerald over 5 s. Wobble PAUSES the fill; it never resets it. Capture frames during the fill.
E. Catch Me (about 10 s): "Now show me your usual 3 pm slouch." Record about 3 s of slouch frames. Turn the outline amber and say "Caught it". Then "Back to your reset" and confirm it returns to emerald. If no slouch is seen after 6 s, say "Totally fine, we'll learn it as you work" and continue with default thresholds.
F. Glow-up reveal: overlay of slouch outline vs reset outline (outlines ONLY: no face, no background). Stats written as estimates, for example "Head about 1.8 in higher". Convert pixel changes to inches using the iris scale (11.7 mm horizontal iris diameter / iris width in pixels) at the face plane. Buttons: "Start my first session" and "Share my glow-up" (renders a PNG from the outlines on a canvas, never from camera pixels).

STANDING first run (about 65 s): triggered when the user picks Sit and stand or Stand only, or the first time Desk Sense thinks she is standing with no standing print. Steps: "Raise your desk. I'll wait." (detect the desk move, say "Desk up!"), Standing Setup Check (framing with both elbows visible, screen height: if face pitch is tipped down warn "Your screen sits low for standing", elbows about 90-120 degrees, light), Standing Body Reset ("Feet about hip-width, weight even." / "Soften your knees." / "Roll your shoulders and let them drop." / "Chin straight back." / "Hands on the keyboard, elbows by your sides. Breathe out."), Lock with a 25% larger ring and looser stillness, Catch Me ("leaning on one hip or poking your head forward"), save as the standing print.

DAILY ZEN CHECK: in the first 20 s of the first session each day, silently compare steady, high-confidence frames to the active print. If within the deadband, show a small toast "Zen Check done. Same setup as yesterday." If not, offer "One breath to tune today's print?" (8 s: one breath + 5 s hold) which updates the CURRENT print within the adaptive guardrails below.

ENGINEERING SPEC (all numbers are starting points; put them in one config file, src/calibration/config.ts, so they can be tuned)
1. Features. Split into INTERNAL (body-shape ratios normalized by shoulder width; these do not change when the camera moves) and EXTERNAL (absolute position and scale in the frame; these do change when the camera moves).
   Internal: head drop = (shoulderMid.y - eyeMid.y) / shoulderWidth; lean-in ratio = faceWidth / shoulderWidth; shrug = mean(shoulder.y - ear.y) / shoulderWidth; rounding = shoulderWidth / faceWidth; forward-head proxy = angle at shoulderMid between lines to left ear and right ear (pose landmarks 7, 8, 11, 12); head roll from the Face Landmarker facial transformation matrix; standing extras: hip lean = (hipMid.x - shoulderMid.x) / shoulderWidth, elbow angles from landmarks 11-13-15 and 12-14-16.
   External: face scale (iris diameter in px), head center x/y, face pitch and yaw, shoulder width in px.
   Reuse existing signal code where it exists; add only what is missing.
2. Per-frame quality gates: pose presence >= 0.5; both shoulders visibility >= 0.6; exactly one face (if more than one, use the largest and pause if a second face is close in size); |yaw| <= 15 deg relative to the print's yaw; |roll| <= 10 deg; frame-to-frame nose motion < 1.5% of frame width; face luminance in a sane range (sample a 64x48 downscaled canvas of the face box, on-device only).
3. Capture: process at about 15 fps during calibration. Lock = 5 s (about 75 frames). Require >= 45 good frames; if fewer, extend up to 10 s; if still fewer, fail with ONE specific fix ("Your face is in shadow. Face a window or lamp.").
4. Aggregation: per feature use a 20% trimmed mean (drop lowest and highest 20%) as the value, and MAD (median absolute deviation) x 1.4826 as the spread. If fewer than 30 good frames, use the median. Keep the existing median code as the fallback path.
5. Personal deadband per feature = max(existing default threshold floor, 3 x spread).
6. Two-point thresholds from Catch Me: slouchDelta = slouchValue - baselineValue (per feature). threshold = baseline + k x slouchDelta with k = 0.5, clamped between the deadband and the existing default threshold. If |slouchDelta| < 2 x spread for a feature, set that feature's weight to 0.5 (the camera can't see it well for this person). Store the weights.
7. Confidence score 0-100 = 30 x goodFrameRatio + 20 x stillness + 20 x mean landmark visibility + 15 x lighting + 15 x framing/angle (each term 0..1). >= 75 save. 55-74 save and show one tip. < 55 retry with the specific fix.
8. Posture Print data model (extend existing storage, keep keys): { version, mode: "sit" | "stand", deskProfileId, createdAt, updatedAt, anchor: {values, spread}, current: {values, spread}, slouchDelta, weights, confidence, external: {faceScale, headX, headY, pitch, yaw, shoulderWidthPx}, camera: {deviceId, label, width, height} }. Separate prints per mode per desk profile.
9. Adaptive baseline guardrails: only learn from VERIFIED good moments (score in the top band for >= 20 s with confidence >= 75, or right after the user corrects following a nudge and holds 10 s, or the "That's my good posture" button with a 3 s hold). Update current = current + 0.05 x (sample - current). Never let current drift from anchor by more than 35% of slouchDelta, and never more than 15% toward the slouch side. Show a small "Print tuned" note when it changes. Settings > Reset to my original print.
10. Setup-change detector (auto re-anchor): if EXTERNAL features step-change (more than 40% of slouchDelta-equivalent within 3 s) and stay changed for 20 s while INTERNAL features stay within their deadbands, treat it as a camera move: silently re-anchor the external part of the print and keep nudging. If both internal and external change, pause nudges and show "Looks like your camera moved. 10-second re-check?" If navigator.mediaDevices reports a different deviceId or resolution, ask "New desk? Give it a name." and create a desk profile.
11. Desk Sense (sit/stand): (a) transition event = head moves more than 25% of frame height within 3 s or leaves the frame, OR the background slides: use the pose segmentation mask (outputSegmentationMasks: true) to ignore the person, downscale to 80x60 grayscale, take row-mean profiles every 150 ms, cross-correlate with the previous profile to get vertical shift; a same-direction shift that accumulates past 10% of frame height within 20 s means the desk/camera moved. (b) After a transition, pause nudges, wait for 3 s of stillness, then compute a weighted distance from current INTERNAL features (plus elbow angle, hip visibility, torso-to-shoulder-width ratio when hips are visible, and sway = std of high-passed nose position over 10 s / shoulderWidth) to BOTH the sitting and the standing prints, scaled by each print's spread. (c) Switch when one print wins by a margin for 15 s (hysteresis). Without a transition event, only switch if the margin holds for 60 s. (d) The first 3 switches ask "Standing now?" Yes/No and store the answers as labels; after that show "Standing mode on" with Undo. Keep a manual toggle and a keyboard shortcut. Remove the old rule that compares face/shoulder height in the frame to the sitting baseline (it fails when the camera rises with the desk), but keep it as one low-weight input if useful.
12. "Too strict? Let's tune it": trigger when the user dismisses 3 nudges within 60 minutes or taps "I wasn't slouching". One question with three answers: "I was reading up close" (raise lean-in threshold 15%), "I was looking at another screen" (store a yaw zone and ignore it), "It's just too picky" (raise all thresholds 15%). Cap total loosening at +50%.
13. Edge cases: glasses (fall back to face width for scale if iris confidence is low or glare is present), backlight, low laptop camera (show a laptop stand tip), side-mounted camera (store baseline yaw up to 25 deg), second monitor (yaw zones), long hair or headphones covering ears (drop the ear-based features and reweight), hoodies or dark chairs (lower shoulder visibility threshold to 0.5 but lower confidence), wheelchair users and limited mobility (Skip movements; hide "taller" stats), multiple people, slow laptops (drop to 8-10 fps while monitoring), reduced-motion setting (no orb animation, show a progress bar).
